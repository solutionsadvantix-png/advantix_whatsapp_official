How It Works - Native Odoo Workflow

No need to open Meta Business Manager! Manage your entire WhatsApp messaging directly inside Odoo via our custom dedicated WhatsApp App Menu.

1

One-Time Account Setup

Ensure you have an active Meta WhatsApp Business Account. Open the new WhatsApp main menu in Odoo, go to Configuration > WhatsApp Account, enter your Meta Phone Number ID and Permanent Access Token, and save.

2

Create Templates Directly Inside Odoo

Forget switching tabs to Meta Business Suite! Go to WhatsApp > Templates inside Odoo and click New. Choose your target model (e.g., Sales Order or Journal Entry for Invoices) and write your message using Odoo field expressions:
Hello {{object.partner_id.name}}, thanks for paying your invoice {{object.name}}.

3

Push to Meta for Instant Approval

Click the Send to Meta button in your Odoo template view. The module automatically converts your variables to Meta's positional {{1}}, {{2}} syntax, cleans formatting, pulls dynamic real sample records, and registers the template with Meta API instantly without manual entry.

4

1-Click Send & Automatic Chatter Logging

Open any Sales Order or Invoice, click the Send WhatsApp action button, and hit Send. The message is delivered immediately to the recipient and logged right inside the Odoo record's Chatter history for seamless tracking.
